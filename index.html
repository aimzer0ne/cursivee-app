What is a cursive text generator?

A cursive text generator swaps each letter you type for a Unicode script character — 𝒜, 𝓑, 𝒸. They are characters, not a font, so the cursive survives being pasted into apps that have no font menu.

Bios, captions, comments, display names and messages on Instagram, TikTok, Discord, X and WhatsApp all accept it. Usernames do not. Keep hashtags, links and anything people need to search for in plain letters.

FAQ

How do I copy and paste cursive text?

Press Copy beside any style, then paste it into any app as ordinary text. Tap the text itself to add a decoration first.

How do I write my name in cursive?

Type your name in the box and compare the styles. Bold script is the safest choice for most names.

Is this a cursive font generator?

Yes. Technically it makes Unicode characters rather than a font file, which is why the cursive keeps its shape anywhere you paste it.

Why did some letters or numbers not change?

Unicode has no cursive form for them — digits and accented letters pass through unchanged.

Why does it show up as boxes or question marks?

The viewer's device has no font for those characters. Bold script and Old English are the most widely supported.

Is it free and private?

Yes. No account, no limit, and everything runs in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere.
