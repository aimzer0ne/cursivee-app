How to use the cursed text generator

  1. Type your text. Enter it in the box at the top of the page. All eleven styles update as you type.
  2. Choose how cursed. Pick a tier, then use the Intensity dial and the Zones toggles to push the marks above, through or below the letters.
  3. Copy and paste. Press Copy and paste it into a caption, message or post. Keep a plain copy of your text first — the conversion only runs one way.

What is cursed text?

Cursed text is text that looks deliberately damaged. It combines two Unicode effects: the letters are first swapped for an unusual form — small caps, mirrored letters or alternating case — and then buried under stacked combining marks. It is also called creepy text, scary text, demonic text or corrupted text.

Cursed text vs glitch text: glitch text stacks marks on your original letters. Cursed text changes the letters first, and that second layer is what makes it read as cursed rather than just broken. If you want your own letters left intact underneath, use the glitch text generator.

Where cursed text works

  • Works well: the lightly cursed styles in Instagram and TikTok captions, Discord messages, posts on X and most chat apps — ideal for spooky captions, horror stories and Halloween posts.
  • Test first: bios. The lighter styles usually survive there.
  • Avoid: usernames, which reject most of it, and the heavier tiers anywhere with a character limit. They are long, overlap nearby lines and are often clipped or stripped.

Tips and limits

  • It only works one way. Nothing here turns cursed text back into plain letters, so copy your original somewhere safe first.
  • Boxes mean missing fonts. These styles combine an uncommon alphabet with deep mark stacking, so they reach the limits of a device's fonts sooner. Try a lighter style if you see empty boxes.

Cursed text generator FAQ

How do I make cursed text?

Type in the box at the top of the page. Your words appear underneath in eleven cursed styles, from Whispers to Unspeakable. Adjust the Intensity dial for more or less corruption, press Copy, and paste it anywhere plain text is accepted.

What is cursed text?

Text that looks like it has been through something. It is two effects at once: the letters are swapped for an unusual Unicode form, then combining marks are stacked on top. The result reads as deliberately damaged rather than as a font choice or a display fault.

What is the difference between cursed text and glitch text?

Glitch text stacks marks on plain letters. Cursed text changes the letters first — small caps, mirrored forms, alternating case — and then stacks the marks. Use the glitch text generator if you want your original letters intact underneath.

Will cursed text work in my bio or username?

In bios and captions the lighter styles usually survive. Usernames are much stricter and often reject or flatten it. Treat anything from Beyond saving as a post, not a name — it is long, overlaps nearby lines, and moderation systems notice it.

Why does it sometimes show empty boxes?

Your device has no font for that character. These styles combine an uncommon alphabet with deep mark stacking, so they hit the limits of font coverage sooner than anything else here. Try a lighter style.

Is cursed text dangerous?

No. Despite the name it is ordinary Unicode text and cannot carry anything harmful. The worst it does is overlap nearby lines, use up a character limit, or get removed by a filter. It is also very hard for screen readers, so keep it out of anything people need to read.

Can cursed text be undone?

Not on this site. The conversion only runs one way, and once you paste the result the original is gone unless you kept it. Save your plain text first if you will need it again.
