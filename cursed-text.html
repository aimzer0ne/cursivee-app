How to make cursed text you can copy and paste

This cursed text generator differs from an ordinary glitch tool in one specific way: the letters are recast first — into small caps, mirrored forms or broken case — and corrupted afterwards. That second layer is what reads as cursed rather than merely broken. People also call the result creepy text, scary text, demonic text or corrupted text; it is all the same technique.

  1. Type or paste your text into the box at the top of the page.
  2. Choose how cursed. Pick a tier, then use the intensity dial and the zone toggles to push the marks above, through or below the letters.
  3. Press Copy and paste it into a caption, message or post. Keep your plain version somewhere first, because the conversion only runs one way.

Where cursed text works

The lightly cursed styles usually survive in Instagram and TikTok captions, Discord messages, posts on X and most chat apps, which makes them the ones to use for spooky captions, horror stories and Halloween posts. The heavier tiers are long, overlap neighbouring lines, and are often clipped or stripped, and usernames reject most of it. Paste a test before you rely on any of them.

What it is not

It is not reversible. Nothing here translates or decodes cursed text back to plain letters, and once you have pasted the result the original is gone unless you kept it — so copy your plain version somewhere safe first. It is also not a font you can install: nothing on this page produces a file.

Cursed text generator FAQ

How do I make cursed text?

Type in the box at the top of the page. Your words appear underneath in eleven cursed styles, from Whispers to Unspeakable. Adjust the intensity dial if you want more or less corruption, press Copy, and paste it wherever plain text is accepted.

What is cursed text?

Loosely, any text that looks like it has been through something. In practice it is two effects at once: the letters are swapped for an unusual Unicode alphabet, then combining marks are stacked on top. The result reads as deliberately damaged rather than as a font choice or a rendering fault.

What is the difference between cursed text and glitch text?

Glitch text stacks marks on plain letters. Cursed text changes the letters first — small caps, mirrored forms, alternating case — and then stacks the marks. Use the glitch text generator if you want your original letters left intact underneath.

Will cursed text work in my bio or username?

In bios and captions the lighter styles usually survive. Usernames are far stricter and will often reject or flatten it. Anything from Beyond saving is best treated as a post, not an identity — it is long, it overlaps neighbouring lines, and moderation systems notice.

Why does it sometimes show empty boxes?

Your device has no font covering that character. These styles combine two demanding things — an uncommon alphabet and deep mark stacking — so they hit the limits of a font's coverage sooner than anything else here. Try a lighter style, or one of the plain-letter styles rather than a recast alphabet.

Is cursed text dangerous?

No. Despite the name it is ordinary Unicode text, and it cannot carry anything harmful. The worst it does is overlap the lines around it, use up a character limit, or get removed by a filter. It is also very hard going for screen readers, so keep it out of anything people need to read.

Can cursed text be undone?

Not by this site. The conversion only runs one way, and once the text is pasted somewhere the original is gone unless you kept it. Copy your plain version somewhere safe first if you will need it back.

The long way round

👁️ Two sensible rules, stacked into something horrible

There's no trick here. 🕯️ Unicode lets you swap letters for other letters — that's just an alphabet. It also lets a letter carry accent marks — that's just an accent. Both perfectly reasonable on their own. Cursed text is what you get when you do both at once and don't stop.

That's the part worth sitting with, and it isn't really about horror. 🧱 A system can be built entirely out of sensible rules and still end up containing things nobody planned, wanted, or thought to forbid. No one ever voted on whether a single letter should be allowed forty diacritics. Nobody asked, so the answer is yes.

It breaks things in very specific ways, too. ⚰️ The marks take up no width, so the text grows upwards and downwards, wandering quietly into the lines around it while looking perfectly ordinary character by character. A filter counting characters sees a wall. You see three words. You're both right.

So the dial is the real control. 🎚️ Lightly cursed is a mood — a caption that feels a bit off. Beyond saving is a statement, and the kind that gets truncated or silently flattened on arrival. The light settings travel almost anywhere. The heavy ones mostly travel to a moderation queue.

Same warning as always, and it counts double here. 🔇 To a screen reader this isn't atmosphere, it's a long list of accent names with a word hidden somewhere inside. Fine for a post nobody needs to parse. Not fine anywhere the words are the point. Everything here runs in your browser at cursivee.app — nothing is sent anywhere.
