What is useful to include

Most reports come down to a character behaving differently somewhere else than it does here, and those are hard to reproduce blind. Anything to do with cursivee.app reaches the same inbox. If you can, tell us:

  • A broken character — which generator, which style, and the text you typed. A screenshot of how it looked in the app where it went wrong helps enormously.
  • A platform that rejects the output — which service, and whether it stripped the characters, replaced them, or refused the whole entry.
  • A style worth adding — a sample of the characters, or the name of the Unicode block they come from.
  • An accessibility problem — these go to the top of the list.

Things we cannot help with

  • Recovering text you lost. Nothing you type is stored anywhere, including by us, so there is no copy to recover.
  • Getting a username approved. Whether a service accepts these characters is entirely that service's decision.
  • Fonts on your device. Empty boxes mean the device has no font covering that character — that is a system-level gap, not something the site can change.

Reply times

This is a small site run by a small number of people. Expect a few days, and expect a real answer rather than a fast one.
