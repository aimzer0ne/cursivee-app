About cursivee.app

A free cursive text generator.

What it is

cursivee.app is a free cursive text generator — and decorated cursive nicknames. No account, nothing to install, and it works offline.

Letters, not fonts

Unicode includes whole alphabets of script, blackletter, bold and circled letters. This site swaps each letter you type for its counterpart in one of them — they are real characters, so the style survives copy and paste.

Limits

  • Screen readers may read these letters as symbols or skip them. Keep important words plain.
  • Missing letters come through unchanged — some sets are incomplete.
  • Usernames on most platforms reject them.
  • Older devices may show empty boxes.

Privacy

What you type never leaves your device. The only third party is Google Analytics. See the privacy policy.
